About

An independent studio, one point of contact

Dune Lab is a design and development studio based on the Arcachon Bay, in south-west France. It builds websites, mobile apps and custom software for independent professionals, shops and businesses.

The person you talk to is the person who designs and builds your project: no middlemen, no outsourcing, and decisions made together with you.

  • One point of contact

    From the first quote to launch, you always deal with the same person.

  • Local and remote

    Meetings in person around the Arcachon Bay, projects delivered across France and remotely worldwide.

  • Code that lasts

    Proven technologies and clean code, so your project stays easy to maintain and extend.
